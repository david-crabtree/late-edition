Four parallax layers (city → back wall → floor → foreground) scroll at different speeds as you walk, and the room's little lives run on their own — a colleague hammering a back-desk typewriter, rain on the blinds, the wire ticker, a swaying bulb, a cat that won't be rushed. Every limb is drawn as a connected arm, so hands actually land on the keys. In the app these beats are driven by the pipeline's event log.
